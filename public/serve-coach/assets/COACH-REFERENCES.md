# Original Serve Coach references

The six `coach-{toss,trophy,finish}-{right,left}.svg` illustrations were created specifically for Serve Coach. They contain no third-party photography, footage, tracing or likenesses. These original illustrations are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.

Their landmarks are defined in `js/phase-reference.js` so the displayed drawing and numerical comparison use the same coordinates. Left-handed versions mirror the position and swap anatomical landmark indices.

These are schematic examples, not a professional player's measured form or a universal target. Perspective, body proportions and the recording's camera position affect comparisons. They do not depict or establish ball release, ball accuracy, racket speed or landing balance.
